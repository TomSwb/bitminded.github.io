Welcome!

This is the Bitminded launchpad. For now it is a place to show my projects; the goal is to grow into a company that serves people through thoughtfully made software. I started from zero coding knowledge in 2024, moved to vibe-coding in 2025, and 2026 marked the beginning of writing software by hand, with AI used only to learn what I need to know, not to do the work for me.

The way of 0s and 1s

Coding is the way I found to express my creativity. Coding is simple: it works, or it doesn't. 0s and 1s, put together, making your computer do what you want (maybe). I find the beauty of this simple logic soothing, and it allows anyone to do anything.

Projects

I enjoy making two types of projects: useful products and fun video games. As I am only getting started, my goal is to grow my library with projects that train different software development skills, discovering my path as I go.

AI Policy

My journey began in 2024, and at first I used AI for everything of consequence, including the first version of this website. I vibe-coded a whole CRM system and many more tools, until I reached the point of integrating payment methods and starting to sell my products. I could not move forward: I was unwilling to gamble with people's money and data. I decided I needed to learn to do all of it myself, using AI only as an advanced web search. All projects (including this website) are to be considered handmade.
